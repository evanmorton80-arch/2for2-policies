2for2 Support

Last updated: November 25, 2026

For account help, privacy questions, reports or moderation concerns, contact the 2for2 Owner and Privacy Contact at 2for2support@gmail.com.

Include your 2for2 username, the relevant post link or account username, a short description, and your app version/device if relevant. Share only information needed to explain the issue. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, payment details or sensitive betslip screenshots.

Reporting and blocking

Use Post options → Report inappropriate content or the flag beside a comment. Use Report profile on a profile, or email its username and explanation. Reporting does not automatically remove content. Block from a profile or post options; manage blocked accounts in Settings → Blocked accounts.

The individual operator is responsible for reviewing support requests periodically. Support is not staffed continuously and no fixed response time is promised. See the Community Guidelines.

Pending submissions

“Checking or awaiting review” means content is saved privately and not yet available to other users. Automatic free OpenAI checks can approve passing content when enabled; flags, unsupported images, outages and service limits require further review. Free OpenAI text/image moderation is active. Passing submissions can publish automatically; unavailable checks keep content private for owner review. Google Cloud Vision is not used. Image checks do not supply OCR or comprehensive embedded-text screening.

Avoid repeatedly submitting the same slip. Refresh its status or check Settings; duplicate protections retain the original receipt. Proposed profile changes keep the last approved public profile visible. Contact support for a rejected submission, publishing restriction or beta-access request, with the relevant username/post reference and explanation.

Account access and deletion

Email confirmation is currently disabled, and password-recovery email delivery has not been validated. Keep your password secure. A support email does not prove account ownership, and an account cannot be transferred simply because someone requests it.

While signed in, cloud account management offers deletion using your current password and explicit confirmation. Deletion and local-cache limits are explained in the Privacy Policy. Contact support if deletion fails or you cannot access those controls; do not send your password.

Sportsbook questions

2for2 does not accept wagers, process betting payments or manage sportsbook accounts. Tail opens the original independent sportsbook link. For problems with that sportsbook, contact the provider directly. No wager is needed to use 2for2's social features.
