2for2 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who operates 2for2

2for2 is independently operated by its individual owner. It is a social application initially intended for Ontario adults aged 19 and older. It does not accept wagers, process betting payments or hold betting funds. Age is self-declared; 2for2 does not verify identity or physical location.

The 2for2 Owner and Privacy Contact handles privacy questions and requests at 2for2support@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Account and profile: email address, authentication information, username, display name, biography, optional profile image and age attestation. Your account email is not displayed on your public profile.
  • Content and activity: images, captions, shared text and URLs, available link-preview information, comments, follows, likes/dislikes, reposts, Activity notifications and privacy/sharing preferences. Shared links can contain sportsbook share codes; images can reveal personal or wager information and may retain embedded file metadata. Review images and their metadata before uploading.
  • Safety and support: reports, block records, moderation status/revisions, private proposed profile changes, screening jobs, content fingerprints, operator decisions, account restrictions, provider-use counters and information you send to the support mailbox, including your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Device and service information: saved sessions, drafts and image caches support sign-in and interrupted sharing. Hosting and distribution providers may process connection details and operational diagnostics, including IP addresses and device/app information.

We use this information to provide accounts, display chosen content, synchronize social activity, enforce audience/blocking controls, receive reports, respond to requests and operate the service. The current app has no advertising or third-party analytics integration and does not request contacts or GPS access. It does not use the camera or microphone.

Who can see content

Profiles and social activity are visible to eligible community members. Public posts are available to eligible signed-in users, subject to blocking. Followers posts are restricted to their authors and eligible followers. Reposts retain the original restrictions. Reports and block records are not public community lists. The operator has administrative access when needed to operate the service or investigate a concern; this is not end-to-end encrypted messaging.

Images use private storage with temporary signed delivery links. Blocking prevents new eligible access, but previously issued links may work until they expire. Neither blocking nor deletion can recall screenshots or copies someone already saved.

Content safety checks

New posts, comments, profile changes and images remain private while checked or awaiting owner review. The last approved profile remains visible while proposed edits are reviewed. Passing submissions keep their selected audience; a moderation approval does not change a followers-only post to public.

The beta supports the free OpenAI moderation API for text and image safety checks. When enabled, selected content—including followers-only submissions—may be sent to OpenAI: captions, shared text, profile text, comments, available preview text and complete uploaded images. Account email records, passwords, authentication tokens and reporter identities are not included in these requests, but images/text you select can themselves contain personal information. Review them before sharing.

Current processing status: automatic OpenAI checks are not enabled while API access is being configured. New submissions wait for owner review. No content is sent to Google Cloud Vision, and that service is not used.

OpenAI image checks cover supported visual categories such as sexual content, violence and self-harm. They do not provide an OCR transcript or comprehensive screening of text embedded in screenshots. Automated checks can miss violations or flag harmless content; reporting, blocking, owner review and appeals remain available. Unsupported/large images or failed checks stay private rather than being released without review.

Sharing and external links

The iPhone Share Extension receives content supplied by the source app. Image pasting is initiated by you through the system Paste control; the Mac web app supports user-initiated paste, file selection and drag-and-drop. Pending shares can remain on the device for recovery. Automatic publishing requires prior explicit opt-in.

Link previews can contain information supplied by the source app or website. Tail opens the original third-party URL, which may contain a share code or prefill a betslip. That service receives the connection and handles any subsequent account or betting activity under its own policies. 2for2 does not access your private sportsbook account or place a wager.

Providers and hosting

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, image storage and account-deletion processing. The existing project is hosted in the United States, region us-east-1.
  • Apple provides iOS, TestFlight and system sharing/link-preview services. Expo provides application build, distribution and update infrastructure. These providers may process operational or diagnostic information.
  • Google provides the Gmail support mailbox. This is separate from Google Cloud Vision, which is not used.
  • OpenAI is the configured candidate for free text/image moderation when API access is enabled. Its processing can occur outside Canada. The processing-status statement above identifies whether automatic checks are currently active; no general provider retention or immediate-erasure guarantee is made.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these information pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security. These pages add no analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or contact forms.

Provider processing can occur outside Canada. Links to provider policies give additional information about their practices.

Retention and deletion

Cloud content has no general automatic expiry: it can remain while the account/content exists unless removed. Reports, sharing receipts, held/rejected content and moderation records also have no general scheduled automatic expiry. Account/content deletion removes related records according to their database relationships, including reports when their reporter or target is deleted. Aggregate provider-use counters can remain without an account reference. Superseded avatar files can remain privately stored until account deletion. Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox; no fixed retention period has been adopted for it.

You can edit your profile, remove your posts/comments and delete your cloud account from account management using your current password and explicit confirmation. Account deletion removes owned uploaded media, then the authentication account and dependent social records. A failed or interrupted cleanup may require retry; post deletion can remove the post before image cleanup completes.

The app also attempts to remove that account's saved native drafts on the current device after successful cloud deletion. Unassigned or legacy caches, data on other devices, the separate local demo and independent copies can remain. Signing out alone does not erase saved drafts. Native sessions use Keychain storage; web sessions use browser storage.

Deleting live data does not establish immediate deletion from provider logs or backups. Exact provider log/backup retention and erasure deadlines have not been verified, and no fixed deadline is promised here.

Your controls and questions

Use audience settings, reporting and blocking to control your experience. For access, correction, deletion questions or a privacy complaint, contact 2for2support@gmail.com. The operator may need to verify account ownership; do not email passwords or authentication codes. No system can guarantee absolute confidentiality.

This beta does not require email confirmation, and email recovery delivery has not been validated. Protect your password. See the Community Guidelines and Support page. Policy changes will appear here with an updated date.
