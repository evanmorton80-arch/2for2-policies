2for2 Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 25, 2026

2for2 is a social community initially intended for Ontario adults aged 19 and older. You do not need to place a wager to participate. Age is self-declared, not verified.

Share responsibly

  • Share content you created or have permission to share. Do not impersonate people or sportsbooks, falsely claim affiliation, or present fabricated selections, odds or results as verified.
  • Remove account numbers, payment information and other sensitive details from screenshots. Do not post someone else's private information or images without permission.
  • Do not post harassment, hate, threats, exploitation, sexual content, scams, spam or illegal content. Do not encourage violence or self-harm.
  • Do not encourage underage gambling, evasion of regional restrictions, reckless wagering or guaranteed-profit claims. Do not pressure anyone to bet or use 2for2 to collect betting funds.

Tail opens another service and may prefill a third-party betslip. A shared image or preview is not proof of a verified wager, outcome or profit.

Report and block

Use Post options → Report inappropriate content or the flag beside a comment. Choose a reason and include relevant details. Reporting does not automatically remove content. For profile concerns, email the username and explanation to 2for2support@gmail.com.

Block an account from its profile or post options. Unblock through Settings → Blocked accounts. Blocking cannot erase copies someone already saved.

Review and concerns

The individual operator is responsible for periodically reviewing reports and posted content. Moderation is not staffed continuously, and not every post receives human approval before publication. The current beta has no automated content filter or pre-publication review queue.

The operator is responsible for investigating concerns using available administrative controls. Contact 2for2support@gmail.com to raise a concern or question a moderation action. Include the relevant username/post link and explanation; do not send passwords or authentication codes. No fixed response time is promised. See Support and the Privacy Policy.
